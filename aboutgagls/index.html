About GAGLS

Graduate Association of Germanic Languages and Studies (GAGLS) is a graduate student organization within the Germanic Studies Department at UT Austin. GAGLS serves as a resource for graduate students by organizing job talks, graduate meet-ups, mock interviews, and other professional and academic events. It provides a platform for intellectual exchange among UT graduate students interested in Germanic languages, literatures, linguistics, and cultures.

For any questions, please contact us at gagls@austin.utexas.edu.

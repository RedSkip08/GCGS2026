11th Biennial Graduate Conference in Germanic Studies (GCGS 2026)

Connections — Between Languages, Spaces, and Cultures

Sunday, April 19th, 2026 at the University of Texas at Austin

Abstract Submission Deadline: February 1st, 2026

Call for Papers

This year's conference theme, Connections — Between Languages, Spaces, and Cultures, invites participants to explore intersections of languages and cultures within the context of Germanic Studies. Organized by the Graduate Association of Germanic Language Students (GAGLS), the GCGS provides an inclusive platform for scholars at all stages of their academic careers to share their research, exchange ideas, and network with fellow academics. The conference is designed to create a supportive environment for graduate students to participate in a peer conference.

Topics of interest for submission include, but are not limited to:

Language in Practice

Language contact

Multilingual communication

Translation and interpreting

Sociolinguistics

Language Teaching and Language Learning

Language pedagogy and assessment

Multilingualism in educational contexts

Foreign language instruction

Heritage and community language education

Theoretical Perspectives

Intercultural communication

Discourse analysis

Historical Linguistics

Literature and Culture

Transnational and transcultural perspectives

Identity and belonging

Literary constructions of space, memory, and mobility

Submission Guidelines

We welcome abstracts from various fields within or related to Germanic Studies. We encourage proposals for individual or co-authored presentations of 20 minutes, followed by 10 minutes of discussion. The conference languages are English and German. Abstracts of 250-500 words, including references, can be submitted through the abstract submission form by February 1st, 2026.
